This page describes the example instances of the Oncology module. The
complete, automatically generated list of all examples can be found in the
[artifact overview](artifacts.html) (section *Example Instances*).

{:.bg-warning}
**Synthetic data only** — never use real or realistic-looking patient data in
examples.

Highlights:

- **Kim Musterperson** — a continuous sample patient whose
  guideline-conformant course of therapy (ovarian carcinoma) is described on
  the [guidance page](guidance.html); the associated instances (diagnosis,
  procedures, TNM panels, follow-up) are interlinked.
- **Transaction bundles of the organ-specific modules**:
  [breast](Bundle-mii-exa-onko-mamma-example-bundle-1.html),
  [colorectal carcinoma](Bundle-mii-exa-onko-krk-bundle.html) and
  [malignant melanoma](Bundle-mii-exa-onko-melanom-bundle.html) demonstrate
  all associated profiles in a server-consumable form.
- **CUP example bundle** — diagnosis of a cancer of unknown primary with
  best-of resolution after identification of the primary
  ([bundle example](Bundle-mii-exa-onko-cup-bundle.html)).

All example instances are synthetic; similarities with actual disease courses
are coincidental.
