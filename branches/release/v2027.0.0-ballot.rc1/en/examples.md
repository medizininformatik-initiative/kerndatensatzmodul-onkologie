## Examples

This page describes the example instances of the Oncology module. The complete, automatically generated list of all examples can be found in the [artifact overview](artifacts.md) (section **Example Instances**).

**Synthetic data only** — never use real or realistic-looking patient data in examples.

Highlights:

* **Kim Musterperson** — a continuous sample patient whose guideline-conformant course of therapy (ovarian carcinoma) is described on the [guidance page](guidance.md); the associated instances (diagnosis, procedures, TNM panels, follow-up) are interlinked.
* **Transaction bundles of the organ-specific modules**: [breast](Bundle-mii-exa-onko-mamma-example-bundle-1.md), [colorectal carcinoma](Bundle-mii-exa-onko-krk-bundle.md) and [malignant melanoma](Bundle-mii-exa-onko-melanom-bundle.md) demonstrate all associated profiles in a server-consumable form.
* **CUP example bundle** — diagnosis of a cancer of unknown primary with best-of resolution after identification of the primary ([bundle example](Bundle-mii-exa-onko-cup-bundle.md)).

All example instances are synthetic; similarities with actual disease courses are coincidental.
