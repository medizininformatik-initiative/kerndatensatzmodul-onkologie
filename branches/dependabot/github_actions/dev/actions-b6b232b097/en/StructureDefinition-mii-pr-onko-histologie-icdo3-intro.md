This profile describes a histological examination in the context of oncology.

For histologies that are used to establish the primary diagnosis, the ICD-O-3 codings SHOULD be recorded directly in the primary diagnosis.
The present profile is reserved for the case that an ICD-O-3 coding is performed on a histology during the course of the disease. Possible scenarios are the histological confirmation of distant metastases or a histological follow-up of known tumour sites.
Examinations that lead to new cancer diagnoses SHOULD be coded directly as part of the new diagnosis.
